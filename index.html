Welcome to AquaWorld

More Than Just an Aquarium

AquaWorld is a place where the wonders of the ocean come alive. Our aquarium brings visitors closer to marine life through fascinating exhibits, educational experiences and conservation activities designed for every age group.

From tiny seahorses to magnificent sharks, every creature has a story to tell. Our goal is to help people understand the importance of healthy oceans and inspire everyone to protect aquatic habitats.

Our Responsibility

Every Drop Makes a Difference

Healthy oceans support incredible biodiversity, help regulate the climate and provide livelihoods for millions of people. Protecting marine ecosystems starts with understanding how our everyday choices affect the natural world.

AquaWorld encourages visitors to learn about ocean conservation, responsible consumption and the importance of protecting aquatic habitats for future generations.

  • ✓ Reduce single-use plastic whenever possible
  • ✓ Keep beaches, rivers and waterways clean
  • ✓ Respect marine wildlife and its natural habitat
  • ✓ Share knowledge about ocean conservation
